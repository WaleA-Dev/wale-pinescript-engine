HOW IT RUNS

From script to verdict in three moves

01 / TRANSLATE

Paste your Pine

A real parser — not regex — compiles ta.* math to vectorized numpy and your stateful logic (var, :=, if-blocks, strategy.exit brackets) into an event-driven bar loop. Anything it can't honor is flagged, never silently dropped.

02 / REPLAY

Fetch live data, run it

Pull split-adjusted bars from Alpaca's free IEX feed — any US stock or ETF, 1-minute to daily — and replay your strategy through a broker emulator that follows TradingView's documented fill rules.

03 / INTERROGATE

Demand statistical proof

Grid-search parameters in-sample, then attack the result: Monte-Carlo permutation p-values, walk-forward out-of-sample, and a final verdict — VALIDATED, OVERFIT, or POOR.

EXECUTION MODEL

Fills you can audit

Most hobby backtesters fill at the close and call it a day. This engine implements TradingView's broker emulator, rule for rule — so the trade list you see here is the trade list you'd see there.
